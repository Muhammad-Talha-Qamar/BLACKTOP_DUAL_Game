# Realistic cars (garage / player)

Each garage slot uses a different free CC-Attribution GLB from Get3DModels / GetGLB
(OUTPISTON redistributions of Sketchfab-sourced models). All are clean street cars
(no rust / wreckage models).

- `beater.glb` — 2006 Audi S4 (starter sedan)
- `sedan.glb` — 2004 Volvo S60R
- `coupe.glb` — 2003 Renault Clio V6 Renault Sport
- `muscle.glb` — 2015 Chevrolet Camaro Z28
- `sports.glb` — 2014 Chevrolet Corvette C7 Z51
- `gt.glb` — 2005 BMW M3 GTR (NFS MW style)

Sources:
- https://www.getglb.com/category/vehicles/
- https://www.get3dmodels.com/
